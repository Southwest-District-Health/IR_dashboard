Incidence rate refers to the number of new cases of a disease occurring in a defined population within a specified time period, expressed per 100,000 individuals.

Rates are shown only when there were 5 or more cases in a year. Rarely reported conditions, those with fewer than 5 cases in every county and every year, are shown for Health District 3 as a whole.
